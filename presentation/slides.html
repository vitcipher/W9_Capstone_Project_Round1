02 The scenario

06 Answering the fear directly

“AI isn't transparent” — here's what we built so that objection doesn't hold.

  • We cite the failure rate, not just the success rate. AI Index 2026's MortgageTax benchmark: even frontier models don't reliably exceed 69.4% accuracy extracting data from real financial documents. We designed around that number, not around a marketing claim.
  • Every extraction is a draft, never a save. The confidence threshold is set at that same 0.70 ceiling — not a round number picked for looks.
  • Every model call is traced in LangSmith — what it saw, what it returned, why it was or wasn't flagged. Inspectable, not a black box.

09 Risks, named — not buried

What could go wrong, and where we've deliberately drawn the line.

Technical

Document extraction tops out around 70% accuracy on real financial documents — designed around with mandatory human review, not hidden.

Regulatory

Uploaded documents are sensitive financial data — a real GDPR surface (legal basis, DPIA, third-party processor) for Round 2, flagged now rather than later.

Explicitly out of scope

Tenant screening or approval decisions. Colorado's AI Act names housing decisions as a discrimination-risk domain alongside hiring and medical care — this product never decides who gets housing, only helps an owner track their own numbers.

Adoption

Our own customer (slide 04) is price-sensitive and low-tech-sophistication — the MVP stays lightweight on purpose, not because we ran out of time.
